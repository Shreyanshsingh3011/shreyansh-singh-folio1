Celestially Referenced Navigation

Shreyansh Singh is working on a defence-technology approach to celestially referenced, integrity-bounded navigation for defence and autonomous platforms.

The problem

Navigation and orientation systems can become less dependable in environments where conventional magnetic heading or external positioning references are degraded, unavailable or untrusted.

The direction

The work explores celestial references, sensor integration and integrity monitoring as part of a more resilient orientation and navigation architecture for defence, robotics and autonomous systems.

Public company corroboration

Sthapana Technologies publicly describes work on celestial-reference orientation for environments where conventional magnetic heading can become unreliable. View Sthapana Technologies on LinkedIn.
