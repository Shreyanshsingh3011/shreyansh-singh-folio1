Shreyansh Singh & Cloud Energy

Cloud Energy is a clean-energy venture associated with Shreyansh Singh, focused on making renewable-energy capacity digitally reservable and commercially accessible.

What Cloud Energy is building

The model combines renewable generation, battery energy storage systems (BESS), energy-management systems and digital capacity reservation for commercial, industrial and infrastructure users.

Renewable capacity

Solar and hybrid renewable-energy generation structured around long-term customer capacity requirements.

Storage

Battery energy storage designed to improve dispatchability and energy availability.

Digital reservation

A digital layer intended to let customers reserve energy capacity rather than treating clean energy only as a conventional asset sale.

Shreyansh Singh’s role

Shreyansh’s work on Cloud Energy spans business model design, project acquisition and financing strategy, technology, investor positioning and international expansion.
