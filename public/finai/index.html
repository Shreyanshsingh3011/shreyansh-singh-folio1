Agentic finance

Shreyansh Singh & FinAI

FinAI is an AI-native finance operating system developed within the Sthapana Technologies ecosystem, focused on financial intelligence, controllership and governed automation.

What FinAI is designed to do

Financial intelligence

P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, explainable analysis and an AI CFO layer grounded in source records.

Finance operations

AP/AR, reconciliation, procurement, close workflows, cash forecasting and anomaly detection.

Governed agents

Multi-step finance agents with approvals, audit trails, evidence and deterministic calculation boundaries.

Shreyansh Singh’s role

Shreyansh’s work on FinAI focuses on product direction, finance-agent architecture, multi-country finance workflows and the broader goal of building an AI-native finance operating system with stronger controllership and explainability.
