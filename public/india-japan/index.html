Shreyansh Singh’s early cross-border leadership work centred on building connections between the Indian and Japanese business ecosystems.

CEO of Connect India Japan at 21

At age 21, Shreyansh Singh was professionally appointed CEO of Connect India Japan, an India–Japan bilateral business platform. The role was an independent executive appointment rather than a position obtained through family ownership, promoter status or a prior directorship.
